1.0 CORE DOCTRINE: THE SEPARATION OF SOURCE & EFFECT
All game mechanics (perks, masteries, attribute bonuses, etc.) are deconstructed into two fundamental and distinct object types: Sources and Effects.

The Source Object: This object represents the perk or ability itself (e.g., "Keenly Empowered II"). Its primary role is to act as a container and define how and when its associated effects are triggered. It holds the trigger, cooldown, and stacking rules that govern its effects.

The Effect Object: This object represents the actual change in game state (e.g., "Apply a 10% Empower buff"). It defines what happens when a Source is triggered. It contains the specific details of the mechanic, such as the category, valueFormula, and duration.

This separation is the Prime Doctrine of our architecture. It ensures the system is modular, scalable, and easy to debug.

4.0 KEY DOCTRINES & HARVESTING RULES
These are the unbreakable laws governing the refinery process.

The Recency Doctrine: When a conflict exists between an entry in the calculatorPerks array and the perkBuckets array within a source JSON file, the entry from perkBuckets is considered the definitive Source of Truth, as it contains the post-patch data.

The Scaling Doctrine: If a perk's raw data contains a valid ScalingPerGearScore string, always assume it scales. The valueFormula must be constructed to include the * perkMultiplier variable, even if the description text shows a flat number.

The First Law of Combat (No Snapshotting): All damage and effect calculations must be performed by the engine in real-time, at the moment of impact or "tick." Our schemas must store the rules and formulas, not pre-calculated values. Aura-like effects (Distained Infliction) must be modeled as passive ON_EQUIP effects that the engine checks dynamically.

The PvP Doctrine: For the purposes of our PvP-focused simulator:

Any part of a description marked "PvE Only" will be ignored.

Any perk marked "PvP Only" will be treated as always active, and the condition will not be included in our translation.
